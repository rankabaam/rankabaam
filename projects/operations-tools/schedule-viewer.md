# Schedule Viewer

**Category:** Local workflow visualization  
**Status:** Iteratively developed local tool  
**Public materials:** Sanitized case study only — no original code or operational data

## Problem

Operational schedules often originate in complex spreadsheets that are difficult to review quickly when dates, assignments, and statuses change. A read-only, simplified view can make the information easier to scan without rewriting the original file.

## General approach

- Accept a user-selected spreadsheet or structured table for local processing.
- Organize records into a date-oriented overview.
- Differentiate ordinary assignments from records that are pending, changed, or cancelled.
- Offer a print-friendly summary for human review.
- Preserve the uploaded source rather than editing it in place.

## Engineering focus

- Clear information hierarchy and visual status cues.
- Robust treatment of incomplete or inconsistent records.
- Output that is understandable without specialist tools.
- Iterative UI refinement based on usability feedback.
